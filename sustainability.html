Quality & Compliance

Quality Management System

The primary goal of IGPL is to achieve the highest standards of quality in our business unit's practices and operations without compromise. Quality performance is one of the cornerstones of our Company's culture and is considered a personal responsibility of all employees.

Our Quality Management System, certified to ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001:2015 provides a structured framework for ensuring consistent product quality, operational excellence, regulatory compliance, and customer satisfaction. Through regular audits, performance monitoring, and continual improvement initiatives, we enhance our processes, products, and services while responding effectively to the evolving needs and expectations of customers and stakeholders.

IGPL remains committed to fostering a culture of quality, accountability, and continuous improvement, ensuring sustainable growth and long-term value for customers, employees, shareholders, and the communities in which we operate.

Health, Safety and Environment Security

IGPL is committed to providing a safe and healthy workplace for all our Employees, Contract Workers, Visitors and member of Public generally. We are committed to compliance with all statutory regulations and best industry practices related to health, safety and environment.

We shall strive to:

  • Eliminate or minimize Hazards and Risk to Health and Safety.
  • Conduct frequent Audits, Risks Assessment and Mock Drill Etc.
  • Educate Employees and Workers for their role and responsibility towards Health and Safety while working in the plant.
  • Provide appropriate Safety equipment and personnel protective equipment.
  • Provide information, instruction and training to enable all Employees, Contract employees, Supervisors and others to conduct themselves safely.
  • Take into account the health and safety performance during career advancement, annual report, Equipment's up gradation and selection or substitution of material.

Environmental Policy

The Company understands the profound influence of industrialization on environment and recognizes the importance of protecting and maintenance the same. In our effort to keep to our commitment for a pollution free environment.

We shall strive to:

  • Prevent and control pollution and maintain ecofriendly environment.
  • Dispose of inevitable wastes in an environmentally friendly manner.
  • Enhance environmental awareness amongst all employees, contract workers, visitors and member of public generally.
  • Continually improve our Environmental performance. Conserve key resources like Water, Energy, Fuel by optimizing their use. Maximize reuse/recycle wastes.
  • Comply with all applicable environmental legislations and other requirements.

This policy shall be communicated to all the managers, staff, workmen, suppliers and regular visitors through display, training and personal interactions and shall be made available to all interested parties and public on demand.

Our Approach

Efficiency Is Our Sustainability Strategy

IGPL's core sustainability lever is the Von-Heyden Low Energy Process. Developed by Wacker Chemie, this catalytic vapour-phase oxidation process is among the most energy-efficient routes to Phthalic Anhydride globally. Running this process at five plants across decades of operation is itself an environmental achievement — the same throughput requires less energy per tonne than older generation processes.

by-product recovery is the second pillar. Maleic Anhydride, Benzoic Acid, and Di-ethyl Phthalate are not waste streams — they are fully recovered products that would otherwise be combusted or discharged. IGPL's by-product recovery systems extract economic and environmental value from every step of the oxidation reaction.

The third pillar is operational continuity. Stable, continuous operations reduce startup and shutdown emissions, minimise off-spec production, and enable precise process control. Our DCS-managed plants run with instrumentation-level oversight across every critical parameter.

Environmental Performance

Where We Focus

Low Energy Process

Von-Heyden Low Energy Process requires less steam and cooling per tonne than earlier-generation fixed-bed processes. Five plants. Same process discipline.

by-product Recovery

Maleic anhydride, benzoic acid, and DEP are fully recovered rather than combusted. Zero waste co-product philosophy across the entire oxidation sequence.

Effluent Treatment

Dedicated effluent treatment plants at Taloja campus. Discharge standards maintained per MPCB and CPCB norms at all times.

Stack Emission Control

Continuous online monitoring of stack emissions. Gas cleaning systems on all process lines. Regular third-party environmental audits.

Water Management

Closed-loop cooling circuits where applicable. Water recycling and recirculation across the campus. Water use per tonne tracked against operational benchmarks.

Occupational Safety

Process safety management program covering all five PA plants. HAZOP-reviewed operations. Safety incident tracking and near-miss reporting systems.

Process Technology

The Wacker Chemie Von-Heyden Process

The core of IGPL's production system is the catalytic vapour-phase oxidation of ortho-xylene over a vanadium pentoxide catalyst. The Von-Heyden Low Energy Process variant reduces heat input requirements compared to earlier designs, enabling the same PA yield at lower per-tonne energy intensity.

Heat recovery systems integrated into reactor design

Continuous DCS monitoring eliminates batch-start emissions

Catalyst management program extends run life and reduces change-out frequency

Regulatory Compliance

Operating Within — and Ahead of — Requirements

MPCB Compliance

Maharashtra Pollution Control Board norms for effluent, emissions, and hazardous waste. Regular third-party environmental audits conducted and results submitted to regulatory authorities.

CPCB Standards

Central Pollution Control Board standards applied across all operational parameters. Consent to Operate maintained and renewed through statutory processes for all five plants.

Factory Compliance

Factories Act compliance, periodic safety audits, and statutory reporting maintained across all five plants. Occupational health surveillance programs in place for all personnel.

Compliance data and environmental reports are available to regulatory authorities on request. IGPL publishes statutory disclosures as required under Indian environmental law.
